### Three pillars

**1. Elegant map-based search**
Buyers move the map and the results move with it. Filter by price, bedrooms, type, amenities or draw their own area. Clusters, instant previews, saved searches and side-by-side comparison are built in. Neighbourhood context (schools, transit, shops) appears on every listing.

**2. Virtual tours that stay connected to the map**
Upload 360° photos, link rooms with arrows, and the tour keeps a live mini-map with a direction cone, or a floor plan with a "you are here" marker. Share by link or QR, embed on any site, or fall back to Matterport/Kuula for existing tours.

**3. A routing engine that never drops a lead**
Define rules by city, locality, price band, property type, language, source or a polygon drawn on the map. Choose round-robin, least-loaded, weighted or listing-agent. Respect working hours and capacity. If a broker doesn't respond in time, the lead is reassigned automatically and escalated to you as a last resort. Every decision is logged and explainable.

### Built to be sold under your brand

* Your domain, logo, colours, fonts and imagery. Each client gets their own site.
* One platform, many clients: separate data, teams, plans and billing.
* A modern admin and broker workspace: dashboard, kanban pipeline, listing manager, tour editor, CSV import.
* Lead notifications by email and webhook; WebInOrbit Analytics built in.

### How it works

1. **Brand it.** Pick colours, upload a logo, set your market.
2. **Load it.** Import listings from CSV or add them with a map pin and drag-and-drop photos.
3. **Tour it.** Upload 360° photos and connect the rooms.
4. **Route it.** Pick your rules, test them in the simulator, go live.

### Who it is for

* Real estate brokerages and developers who want their own portal instead of renting space on a marketplace.
* Agencies with several offices, territories or languages.
* Property marketplaces and franchise networks who need many branded sites on one platform.

### FAQ

**Do we need a Google Maps licence?** No. The platform uses MapLibre and OpenStreetMap. Plug in your preferred tile provider for production traffic.

**Can we use our own domain?** Yes (Pro and above). Point a CNAME at us and we handle the rest.

**Where is our data stored?** In your own PostgreSQL database and storage bucket if you self-host; in a dedicated WebInOrbit environment if managed.

**Can we bring existing 360° tours?** Yes: add a Matterport, Kuula or similar link to any listing.

**How do leads reach our CRM?** Email, the built-in pipeline, and a webhook (Enterprise) with the full lead and routing payload.

**Can brokers use it on mobile?** Yes, the whole workspace is responsive.
